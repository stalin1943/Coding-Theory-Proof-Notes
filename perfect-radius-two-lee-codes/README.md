# Nonexistence of Perfect Two-Error-Correcting Lee Codes

Website: [Proof note](https://stalin1943.github.io/Coding-Theory-Proof-Notes/perfect-radius-two-lee-codes/)

Hello from Yang Shifu, a long-time researcher and enthusiastic traveller in sequence coding, coding theory, and finite-field combinatorics.

Author: **Yang Shifu**

ORCID: [0009-0002-2768-8506](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-2768-8506)

Affiliation: National Mobile Communications Research Laboratory and the Frontiers Science Center for Mobile Information Communication and Security, Southeast University, Nanjing 211189, China.

This is the first public release, version 1.0. The PDF is a single-column proof note with the main argument, the load-bearing lemmas, and the sources.

This note proves that perfect two-error-correcting Lee codes do not exist in $\mathbb{Z}^n$ for any $n\ge 3$, without assuming linearity, periodicity, or a lattice structure.
